Privacy Policy

This policy explains how BISTROALGO handles information when you use the iPhone app, visit this website or contact support.

Effective date: November 3, 2026

In short: the BISTROALGO app has no account, advertising, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs. The app makes no network requests. Your app records and attachments are stored locally and are not transmitted to the developer.

1. Summary

BISTROALGO is a household record-keeping app for iPhone. The developer does not operate a user database, account system, analytics service or cloud storage service for the app. You can use the app without providing your name, email address or any other information to the developer.

2. Information stored on your device

BISTROALGO stores the information you enter in the app's private storage on your iPhone. Depending on how you use the app, this may include:

  • Account names, account types, opening balances and transfers.
  • Household member names, roles and monthly contributions.
  • Income and expense titles, dates, amounts, categories, notes, tags and payment assignments.
  • Recurring bills, merchants, due dates, budgets and related notes.
  • Shared-cost and settlement records.
  • Currency, goals, display, privacy-mask and haptic preferences.
  • Receipt, statement and bill images that you choose to attach.

This information is used only to provide the app's local record-keeping, calculations, charts and export features. The developer cannot access this locally stored information through the app.

3. Camera and photo library access

Device permissions are requested only for features you choose to use:

  • Camera: to photograph a receipt, statement or bill and attach it to your records.
  • Photo library read access: to attach an image that you select.
  • Photo library add access: to save a copy of an attachment to your photo library only when you choose “Save to Photos”.

Attached images are copied into the app's private media storage. The app does not upload them. You can deny or revoke these permissions in iOS Settings; manual record-keeping remains available without them.

4. Exports and sharing

You can create CSV files containing selected app records. Files are generated locally. A file leaves the app only when you choose an action or destination in the iOS share sheet, such as saving it to Files or sending it through another app.

Once you share or save an export outside BISTROALGO, its handling is governed by the destination service and your own settings. Review exports before sharing because they may contain sensitive financial and household information.

5. Data collection, analytics and third parties

The app itself does not collect or transmit personal data to the developer. It does not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs; it does not use your data for advertising or cross-app tracking; and it does not sell or rent personal information.

The app does not connect to banks or financial institutions. Calculations are based on records you enter and are not verified against external accounts.

6. Retention and deletion

App records remain in local app storage until you edit or delete them, use the “Delete all records” control, or remove the app and its data. You can manage records under More → Data management.

Deleting app data cannot be undone from within the app. Deleting the app does not delete CSV files, exported images or other copies that you previously saved elsewhere. Those copies must be managed separately.

7. Security and device backups

Local storage reduces exposure to online services, but no storage method is completely risk-free. Protect your iPhone with a passcode and keep iOS up to date. The optional privacy mask hides amounts on screen but is not encryption or a substitute for device security.

The developer provides no cloud backup or sync service. Apple may include app data in a device backup depending on your iPhone and iCloud settings; that processing is governed by Apple's terms and privacy policy. Export and securely store your own copies if the records matter to you.

8. Support communications

If you email fahim141052@gmail.com, the developer receives your email address, message and any information or attachments you choose to send. This information is used to respond to your request, troubleshoot issues and maintain support records for as long as reasonably necessary.

Please do not send passwords, full bank details or unnecessary financial records. Your email provider processes support messages under its own privacy terms.

9. This website

This informational website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. It is hosted using GitHub Pages. When you visit the site, GitHub may process standard technical information, such as your IP address and request logs, to deliver and protect the service. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement for details.

10. Children's privacy

BISTROALGO is not directed to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. Because app records stay on the user's device, parents and guardians should supervise use and sharing by children.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app, website or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Material changes may also be communicated through the app or its App Store listing where appropriate.
