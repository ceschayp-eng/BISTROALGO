A complete household console

Know what came in, what went out and what comes next.

Every figure comes from records you control. Explore interactive charts, adjust your own targets and keep everyday finances understandable.

01

Overview

See cash flow, spending mix, savings rate and budget progress together. Drag across timelines, tap chart segments and open daily spending details.

02

Income & expenses

Record transactions with categories, accounts, notes, tags and receipt photos. Search and filter your history without connecting a bank.

03

Bills & budgets

Organise recurring bills, see upcoming commitments and set monthly category limits. Track your pace against goals that you define.

04

Household & accounts

Keep account balances, member contributions and shared costs in one place. Settlement figures help work out who should pay whom.

05

Receipts & attachments

Use the camera or choose an image from your photo library to attach receipts, statements and bill documents. Images remain in the app's private storage.

06

CSV exports

Create local CSV copies of expenses, income, bills, budgets, transfers and monthly cash flow. Nothing is shared until you choose a destination.

Your records stay yours

No sign-in. No analytics. No advertising.

BISTROALGO works entirely on your iPhone. The app makes no network requests and sends no financial records or photographs to us.
