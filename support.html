Getting started

  1. Open BISTROALGO and complete the introduction.
  2. Choose the sample household to explore, or start with an empty workspace.
  3. Add household members and accounts before assigning shared transactions.
  4. Record income, expenses, bills and budgets from the relevant tabs.
  5. Set your currency and financial targets under More.

Sample data is generated example content, not a real household's information.

Export your records

  1. Open More and scroll to Export.
  2. Choose expenses, income, recurring bills, budgets, transfers or monthly cash flow.
  3. Tap the share button that appears.
  4. Choose a secure destination in the iOS share sheet.

CSV files can be opened by many spreadsheet apps. Exports are created on your iPhone and are shared only when you choose a destination.

Camera and photo access

If attaching or saving an image does not work, open iOS Settings → BISTROALGO and review Camera and Photos access. You can also check permissions under Settings → Privacy & Security.

The app uses these permissions only to attach images you choose or save an attachment when you tap “Save to Photos”. Denying access does not prevent manual record-keeping.

Privacy and hiding amounts

To conceal figures on screen, open More → Preferences and turn on Privacy mask. Turn it off using the same control.

The privacy mask is a display feature, not a lock screen. Protect the device with an iPhone passcode. BISTROALGO has no account, sign-in, analytics, advertising or developer-operated cloud storage.

Delete or reset app data

Open More → Data management. You can restore the sample household or delete all records. Both actions replace current app data and require confirmation.

Deleting records cannot be undone in the app. Export any records you want to keep first. Previously exported files and images saved outside the app are not removed.

Reinstalling, backup and sync

There is no BISTROALGO account or developer-operated cloud backup. Removing the app may permanently remove its local records. Export important records before deleting or reinstalling the app.

Apple device backups depend on your own iPhone and iCloud settings. The app does not provide multi-device sync, and a CSV export is not an in-app restore backup.

Calculations and bank connections

BISTROALGO does not connect to banks, move money or verify transactions. All totals, projections, budget progress and settlement figures are calculated from information you enter.

If a figure looks wrong, check the underlying transaction, account assignment, date, recurring-bill cycle and currency settings. Check important figures against official statements.

Troubleshooting checklist

  • Confirm you are using an iPhone with iOS 16.0 or later.
  • Restart the app and, if needed, restart your iPhone.
  • Check that your device has available storage.
  • Install any available app or iOS updates.
  • Do not delete the app while troubleshooting unless you have secured any records you need.

Before emailing: never send your device passcode, passwords or full bank credentials. Avoid attaching financial records unless they are necessary to explain the problem.
